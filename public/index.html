Safety & Scam Protection Guide

Essential rules for safe accommodation searching in Soweto

📞 1. Always Call & Verify First

Never rely solely on text or anonymous messages. Speak with the landlord directly via voice call to confirm the street address, monthly rent, prepaid electricity arrangements, and viewing times.

🚫 2. Never Send Money Upfront

Scammers often demand "holding deposits", "viewing fees", or "key courier fees" via e-Wallet or instant cash send before showing you the room. Legitimate landlords will never ask you to pay before an in-person viewing.

👥 3. Always Visit with 1 or 2 People

For your personal safety, always take 1 or 2 friends or family members when going to view any room or backroom. Visit during daytime hours and inform someone close to you of your destination.

🔍 4. In-Person Inspection Checklist
  • Check that door locks, burglar bars, and windows are intact and secure.
  • Confirm if the room has its own prepaid electricity meter or if power is shared.
  • Verify hot water supply, bathroom plumbing, and perimeter gate safety.
  • Ensure a written lease or receipt is provided when you eventually make a payment.
